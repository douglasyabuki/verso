# Resume Pro

Produce usable, persuasive resumes that preserve the candidate's facts and voice. Improve the representation of experience, never the experience itself.

## Invocation and default focus

Use this skill only when the user explicitly invokes `$resume-pro`, selects it, or directly asks to use Resume Pro. Resume-related subject matter alone does not authorize activation; another agent must not activate it implicitly through delegation.

Default to en-US wording, spelling, and US employment-resume conventions. Use pt-BR resume wording and Brazilian conventions only when requested. Portuguese source material, a Brazilian name, or candidate nationality alone does not request that switch. Explicit target-country and submission instructions override market defaults; keep language and country separate. Do not require a locale question when the defaults suffice.

## Essential constraints

- Never invent or inflate employers, titles, dates, qualifications, tools, proficiency, responsibilities, metrics, or results. Distinguish personal contribution from team outcomes. Treat unconfirmed details as questions, not resume facts.
- Preserve the candidate's level of responsibility and natural professional voice. Do not impose corporate superlatives or homogenize every bullet.
- Never use hidden text, keyword stuffing, copied job descriptions, or instructions to manipulate AI reviewers. Offer truthful rewrites instead. Promise neither interviews nor automated-screening success; do not invent match percentages or infer rejection causes from speed.
- Do not upload candidate documents to third-party scoring/parsing services without explicit authorization. Drafting a resume does not authorize applying or contacting employers.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the requested operation and use existing context. Identify target role, seniority, relevant evidence, and submission instructions; apply the language/market defaults unless overridden. Ask only for missing information that materially changes the result; proceed on independent work. If no posting exists, use the target role and disclose consequential assumptions.
2. Distinguish employment resumes (including the UK employment CV and Brazilian currículo) from academic CVs, Lattes, federal/public-sector forms, medical or creative applications, and prescribed formats. For specialized work, consult current institutional or professional-body instructions before applying general resume advice.
3. Read [content and tailoring](references/content.md) when drafting, reviewing, selecting evidence, or tailoring. Keep a working fact ledger from supplied material; note uncertainties and contradictions separately. For tailoring, map required and preferred criteria to evidence and genuine gaps before rewriting.
4. Read [markets and localization](references/markets.md) when requested localization, personal details, or qualification meanings affect the result. Brazilian guidance is conditional on a requested pt-BR/Brazilian focus, not part of every default US resume task.
5. Read [layout and processing](references/layout-processing.md) for formatting, file delivery, ATS advice, or AI-screening questions. Use available document/PDF skills for production and their verification workflows; do not duplicate those mechanics here.
6. Verify changed claims against the fact ledger, relevance against the target, chronology, voice, requested language variant, legibility, and file behavior. Catch plausible but unsupported additions, not just invented numbers. Read [sources](references/sources.md) for consequential advice; cite sources in accompanying advice, never inside the candidate's resume. Recheck current primary documentation for new platform claims or materially different markets.

## Deliverables

- **Review:** prioritize factual/eligibility problems, missing relevant evidence, and reading/extraction problems; provide actual rewrites where evidence permits. Separate essential corrections from optional style choices.
- **Draft/rewrite:** deliver usable resume text. Keep unresolved questions/placeholders separate and explicitly label any incomplete draft. Submission-ready means no unresolved essential facts or unverified file problems.
- **Tailoring:** deliver revised content plus a compact requirements-to-evidence/gaps map when useful. Explain major moves/removals briefly; do not replace the resume with a critique.
- **Formatting/file work:** provide the requested artifact and report visual/text checks actually performed, fixes, and limits. A plain-text rewrite alone does not satisfy a requested file.

For maintenance or regression evaluation, use the realistic prompts and observed results in [validation](references/validation.md). Load this only when evaluating the skill.
